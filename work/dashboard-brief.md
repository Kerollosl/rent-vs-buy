# Dashboard surface brief

- Mode: Operate. A prospective buyer or renter needs to understand which strategy produces more net worth, why, and how sensitive that answer is to assumptions.
- Outcome: Adjust a compact default model, inspect the cash-flow-symmetric comparison immediately, then save, share, or export the scenario. The result must distinguish expense, wealth transfer, and wealth creation.
- Direction: The Closing Table — a contemporary closing folio with cotton-paper surfaces, deep evergreen ink, rust annotations, tabbed navigation, fine rules, and a decisive comparison page. The memorable moment is the winner sentence rewriting itself as assumptions move.
- Scope: Complete responsive single-page application with calculator controls, summary metrics, seven chart views, annual table, presets, local saved scenarios, two-scenario comparison, CSV/config export, advanced assumptions, sensitivity heatmap, and an honest Historical Backtest placeholder.
- States and ranges: 5–40-year horizons; realistic housing, rent, and return ranges; empty saved-scenario state; local-save confirmation; invalid numeric input prevention; negative and positive net-worth differences; no-crossover state.
- Layout: Sticky tabbed assumptions folio on desktop, collapsible sheet on mobile; verdict and paired summaries before charts; table and sensitivity follow in quieter sections. Keyboard focus and non-color line styles remain visible.
- Constraint: React/TypeScript/Vite, local-only data, deterministic MVP calculations, no fabricated historical market data, no hidden principal-as-expense error.
